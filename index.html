What is a pearl candle?+

A pearl candle uses loose candle pearls instead of a single solid candle. Pour them into a suitable heat-safe vessel and add a wick following the product instructions to create your own candle arrangement.

How much comes in a box?+

Each Black Sand Pearl Candle contains 500 g (17.6 oz) of candle pearls.

How long does it burn?+

The supplied product information gives up to approximately 120 hours per 500 g, depending on how the candle is used. Your vessel, wick arrangement and burning routine can affect burn time.

Can I refresh my candle?+

Yes. Once the candle has completely cooled, you can relight the same wick or replace it to refresh your candle, following the instructions supplied with your product.
